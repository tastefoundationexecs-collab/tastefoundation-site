# Teaching • Agriculture • Sustainability • Transformation • Empowerment

We are building a stronger, healthier Detroit — and a more food-secure America — through community agriculture, aquaponics innovation, affordable housing, and economic empowerment.

## Our Journey

T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION began in Sun Lakes, Arizona in 2020, serving unhoused individuals with food, water, and compassion in extreme desert conditions. After local restrictions prevented continued feeding, Founder Sandra Getter relocated to Detroit in 2026, where the Foundation was newly registered and reborn with expanded purpose.

Today, in partnership with Detroit native Julian Tiggs of GETTIN2DAROOTZ INC., T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION is building a multi-site ecosystem of food sovereignty, aquaponics innovation, affordable housing, youth empowerment, and economic uplift — from Detroit neighborhoods to the White Mountains of Georgia.

## Featured Initiatives

- Detroit Community Garden & Greenhouse Campus  
- Detroit Aquaponics Hub  
- White Mountains Commercial Fish Farm  
- Affordable Housing & Real Estate Development  
- Men of Valor Youth Programs
