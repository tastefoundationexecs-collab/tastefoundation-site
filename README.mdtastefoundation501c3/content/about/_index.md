# About T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION CORPORATION

T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION CORPORATION is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit originally registered in Sun Lakes, Arizona in 2020, and newly registered in Detroit, Michigan in 2026 as part of its expansion into the Midwest.

## Our Name: Two Origins, One Mission

T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION was originally born from the **Trans‑Atlantic Slave Trade Expedition**, a college lecture series and cultural education initiative that honored history, preserved truth, and empowered communities through knowledge.

When COVID‑19 halted the lecture tour in 2020, the organization expanded into humanitarian service — feeding unhoused individuals, distributing water, fruit, and hygiene kits, and supporting vulnerable communities. In 2026, the Foundation relocated to Detroit, where its mission grew into agriculture, aquaponics, community gardens, youth empowerment, and housing for unhoused teens.

Today, T.A.S.T.E. stands for **Teaching • Agriculture • Sustainability • Transformation • Empowerment**, reflecting both our historical roots and our modern humanitarian purpose.

## Mission

To produce sustainable, community-rooted food systems that address domestic food security while creating lasting economic opportunity for underserved communities.

## Vision

A future where every family has access to fresh food, safe housing, meaningful work, and a community that supports their growth.
