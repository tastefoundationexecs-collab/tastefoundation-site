# T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION Historical Origins

T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION began as the **Trans‑Atlantic Slave Trade Expedition**, a college lecture tour and educational initiative focused on truth-telling, historical awareness, and cultural empowerment.

In early 2020, the team visited colleges in Ohio, presenting lectures on the Trans‑Atlantic Slave Trade, its impact on generations, and the importance of historical memory. Shortly after, COVID‑19 brought the lecture tour to a halt — but the calling did not end.

From that interruption came a new direction: T.A.S.T.E. FOUNDATION was formally organized as a 501(c)(3) and shifted into humanitarian service, feeding unhoused individuals, offering water and hygiene support, and standing with those most affected by crisis.

As the work grew, so did the vision — from cultural education to food sovereignty, community gardens, aquaponics, housing for unhoused teens, and youth empowerment programs. Our historical origins remain the foundation of our commitment to justice, dignity, and transformation.
